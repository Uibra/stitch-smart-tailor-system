## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an African-rooted, contemporary bespoke tailoring atelier tailored specifically for high-precision mobile workflows: from bespoke client measurements and fabric curation to fitting milestones and final delivery. 

The aesthetic is grounded, refined, and tactile. It eschews transient tech tropes in favor of an architectural, editorial sensibility reminiscent of luxury couture ateliers in Lagos, Nairobi, and Johannesburg. Every touchpoint conveys bespoke dignity, absolute measurement precision, and timeless bespoke craft. Interactions are purposeful, generous, and tactile, honoring physical fabrics, tailor tape measurements, and garment architecture while maintaining iOS and Android ergonomic excellence.

## Colors
The palette balances deep sartorial forestry tones with hand-spun metallic warmth and natural atelier linens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Apex Green - `#123B32`)**: Used for primary brand moments, primary CTAs, active milestone states, and authoritative top bars.
- **Apex Green Pressed (`#0D2F28`)**: Deepened state for pressed touch targets and active navigation anchors.
- **Thread Gold (`#C3A267`)**: Bespoke accent representing sartorial chalk lines, gold threading, garment seals, and milestone completion marks.
- **Soft Green Surface (`#E7ECE8`)**: Tonal substrate for secondary action chips, metric callouts, and swatch container frames.
- **Atelier Ivory Surface (`#F7F3EA`)**: Warm linen-tinted backdrop for editorial features, client measurement slips, and garment dossier highlights.
- **App Background (`#F5F7F5`)**: Calm, cool off-white ground providing crisp separation from surface elements.
- **Card / Content Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure structural surface for interactive cards, sheets, and modular inputs.
- **Border / Structural Stroke (`#D9E0DA`)**: Precise 1px hairline delimiters for sartorial grid lines and tailoring dividers.
- **Charcoal Typography (`#252B29`)**: High-contrast, warm dark neutral for primary labels, editorial titles, and measurements.
- **Muted Text (`#67716C`)**: Calibrated mid-tone for secondary metadata, tailoring notations, and hints.

### Functional Alerts
- **Success (`#237A52`)**: Measurement verified, fitting complete.
- **Warning (`#A86612`)**: Fabric shortage, adjustment required.
- **Error (`#B4232F`)**: Spec mismatch, out-of-tolerance metric.
- **Information (`#315F91`)**: Logistics transit, courier update.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances the structured, expressive character of **Epilogue** for editorial headlining with the functional, legible geometry of **Manrope** for body reading, forms, and precise measurements.

### Numerical Rigor
All financial charges, yardage volumes, and anatomical tailoring metrics (collar, chest, inseam, sleeve) enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`). This prevents visual shifting during live stepper input, fitting validation, and multi-line tally recalculation.

## Layout & Spacing
Designed from a mobile-first paradigm optimized for native iOS and Android environments:

- **Touch Target Paradigm**: All interactive triggers, inputs, counters, and bottom bar destinations observe an absolute physical minimum hit area of 48px, with primary workflows default-padded to 52px for ease of one-handed salon and workshop operation.
- **Safe Area Insets**: Full structural compliance with mobile hardware cutouts, dynamic islands, and home indicator bars (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), ensuring fixed action docks remain unclipped.
- **Canvas System**: A 4-column fluid mobile grid anchored by 16px screen margins (`margin: 1rem`) and 16px gutters (`gutter: 1rem`). On compact screens below 360px, outer margins gracefully scale down to 12px while maintaining the baseline 4px/8px incremental rhythm for internal stacks.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical atelier material layering—cards of fine linen, swatches, and pressed pattern paper—relying on hairline structural bounds and ambient, warm-tinted soft diffusion.

- **Level 0 (Base Ground)**: `#F5F7F5` plain backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Flat Cards & Modules)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an exact 1px border stroke (`#D9E0DA`). Zero hard drop shadows; flat, architectural discipline.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards / Drag Targets)**: Tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 59, 50, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 59, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Bottom Sheets & Modal Dossiers)**: Elevated sheet surface with 20px top corner radius, bordered top hairline, and low-opacity ambient drop: `0 -8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 59, 50, 0.10)`. Backed by an atelier dim overlay (`rgba(37, 43, 41, 0.45)` with 4px backdrop blur).

## Shapes
The shape language combines crisp craft boundaries with tailored curvature:

- **Mobile Content Cards & Swatch Panels**: 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering a plush, tactile handheld footprint.
- **Form Controls, Search Inputs & Metric Steppers**: 12px corner radius (`rounded`), providing precise, compact geometric enclosures.
- **Interactive Action Buttons (Pill & Primary CTA)**: 12px for form buttons, full pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for status chips, order tags, and filter tokens.
- **Bottom Sheets & Floating Drawer Panels**: Specific asymmetrical geometry: 20px top-left and top-right radius, 0px bottom radius, with a centered 4x36px grab handle (`#D9E0DA`, 9999px radius).

## Components

### Buttons & Primary Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Height of 52px. Solid Apex Green (`#123B32`) background, crisp Atelier Ivory (`#F7F3EA`) Manrope medium text, 12px radius. Active pressed state transitions instantly to `#0D2F28`.
- **Secondary Accent (Thread Action)**: Thread Gold (`#C3A267`) fill or 1.5px outline with `#252B29` text for artisanal upgrades, bespoke monogram additions, and fabric confirmation.
- **Tertiary / Soft**: Soft Green (`#E7ECE8`) fill, `#123B32` text, 12px radius.

### Input Fields & Measurement Steppers
- **Text & Metric Fields**: 50px height, 12px radius, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#D9E0DA` border. Focus state elevates the outline to 1.5px `#123B32` without layout jitter.
- **Anatomical Number Inputs**: Displays tabular numbers aligned with measurement units (e.g., `cm`, `in`). Flanked by 48px tactile plus/minus stepper touch targets.
- **Helper & Validation Labels**: Situated 4px below input, set in Manrope 12px (`#67716C` or `#B4232F`).

### Cards & Garment Dossiers
- **Garment Card**: 16px radius, pure white base, 1px `#D9E0DA` perimeter line, 16px internal padding. Houses client name, order phase badge, fabric swatch preview (44x44px rounded-md), and next milestone.
- **Editorial Linen Card**: Atelier Ivory (`#F7F3EA`) surface with subtle Thread Gold hairline boundary; utilized for bespoke master notes and fitting appointment agendas.

### Bottom Sheets & Modals
- **Atelier Bottom Sheet**: Fixed top corner radius of 20px, white background, dragging capsule handle (36x4px, `#D9E0DA`). Includes sticky bottom action bar honoring bottom safe areas.

### Selection Controls & Status Chips
- **Chips**: 34px height, pill-shaped (9999px), 12px horizontal padding. Unselected: `#F5F7F5` with 1px border. Selected: `#123B32` background with `#F7F3EA` typography.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 22x22px with 6px radius for check boxes; 22x22px circle for radios. Active checkmark rendered in Thread Gold on Apex Green ground.
