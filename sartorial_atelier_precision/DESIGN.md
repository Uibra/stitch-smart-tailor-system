## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the disciplined precision of modern bespoke tailoring fused with high-technology workshop execution. It honors traditional artisanal craft while integrating algorithmic fabric layout and computer-guided cutting (*Yanka Kaya*).

The aesthetic marries an atelier’s tactile warmth with industrial fabrication rigor:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep indigo dyed textiles, warm unbleached parchment pattern sheets, burnished terracotta markings, and sharp chalk lines.
- **Personality:** Authoritative, disciplined, grounded, exact.
- **Audience:** Master patternmakers, workshop supervisors, bespoke tailors, and apprentices operating on tablet cutting tables and mobile floor stations.
- **Design Philosophy:** Tactile-functional precision. Structural grid lines, visible dimensional cues, and monospaced measurement callouts elevate fabric manipulation into an exact science. Interfaces feature bilingual English and Hausa microcopy to ensure native clarity across workshop ranks.

## Colors

The palette directly references physical atelier materials—deep indigo dyes, oiled tracing paper, tailor’s chalk, and oxidized brass shears:

- **Primary (`#1B2A4A` - Deep Workshop Indigo):** Anchor color for primary interfaces, authoritative titles, dominant action buttons, and primary structural frames.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Ochre / Dinkin Allura):** High-energy indicator for cutting paths, primary system highlights, active layout alerts, and urgent workshop actions.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` - Raw Brass):** Accent for embroidery milestones, premium order tiers, and measurement calibration highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F4EE` - Unbleached Parchment / Takarda):** A low-strain, warm background providing high legibility under industrial workshop lighting.
- **Supporting Foundation Tones:**
  - *Slate Charcoal (`#121824`):* High-contrast text and critical readouts.
  - *Muted Chalk Line (`#E2DDD4`):* Structural borders, measurement dividers, and container edges.
  - *Calico Surface (`#FFFFFF`):* Elevated component cards and input surfaces.
- **Production Stage Tokens:**
  - *Cutting / Yanka:* Burnt Terracotta (`#C85A32` on `#FBECE5`)
  - *Sewing / Dinki:* Workshop Indigo (`#1B2A4A` on `#E8EDF5`)
  - *Embroidery / Aiki:* Muted Gold (`#936E20` on `#FAF4E6`)
  - *Fitting / Gwaji:* Deep Cyan Slate (`#1D596B` on `#E6F2F5`)
  - *QC / Tantance:* Forest Sage (`#235C3E` on `#E8F4EC`)

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances sartorial elegance with mathematical manufacturing precision:

- **Epilogue (Headlines & Atelier Identity):** Provides sculptural, architectural presence reminiscent of mid-century fashion ateliers and industrial signboards. Used for high-level order names, client profiles, and workspace module headers.
- **Hanken Grotesk (Body & Operational Prose):** Delivers clean, unhindered readability across instructions, fabric notes, and bilingual glossaries. Retains high legibility on mobile screens under direct overhead workshop lights.
- **JetBrains Mono (Measurements, Geometry & Production Identifiers):** Tabular figures are essential for pattern grading, yardage specs, seam tolerances (`±1.5mm`), cutting coordinates, and production barcodes. Numbers align uniformly down tables to eliminate measurement misreadings during high-speed cutting.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface applies a rigid 8pt spatial baseline to echo the precision of tailor cutting mats:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* 4 fluid columns with `1rem` outer margins. Designed for one-handed operation on the floor, prioritizing oversized tap targets and vertical progress cards.
  - *Tablet / Workstation (768px - 1199px):* 8 fluid columns with `1.5rem` margins, enabling simultaneous viewing of cutting pattern diagrams alongside step checklists.
  - *Cutting Hub Desktop / Projector (≥ 1200px):* 12-column fixed grid with a max width of `1440px` and `2.5rem` margins, structured to accommodate multi-panel layout simulations, grain-line trackers, and nested yardage algorithms.
- **Density Philosophy:** Compact internal component spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) for dense measurement grids paired with generous structural separation (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between distinct garments and customer batches.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, architectural boundaries inspired by pinned pattern paper over cutting tables rather than deep blurred software shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Chalk Lines:** Primary elevation is achieved via structured 1px borders (`#E2DDD4`) on `#FFFFFF` surfaces against the parchment base (`#F7F4EE`).
- **Tactile Atelier Shadow (Tonal Offset):**
  - *Resting Cards & Measurement Slates:* `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.05), 0 1px 1px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.02)`.
  - *Active Cutting Sheets & Focus Layers:* `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Floor Modals & Barcode Scanners:* Crisp 2px solid primary borders paired with an ambient indigo-tinted drop: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(27, 42, 74, 0.16)`.
- **Active State Highlights:** Selected pieces in the nesting hub employ an active outer perimeter stroke of `2px solid #C85A32` accompanied by a warm tinted background fill (`#FDF5F1`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is intentionally crisp and tailored, reflecting shears slicing along straight grain lines:

- **Corner Radius Scale:**
  - Base corners use a controlled `0.25rem` (Soft) radius to preserve sharp industrial lines without appearing aggressive.
  - Cards, panels, and modal containers use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem`).
  - Production badges and status chips use `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem`) or square corners with chamfered aesthetics to retain tag-like physical characteristics.
- **Structural Lines:** Horizontal and vertical separators mimic basting thread or chalk rulers with deliberate, thin continuous borders (`1px solid #E2DDD4`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action / Yanka Yanzu):** Deep Workshop Indigo background (`#1B2A4A`), sharp parchment text, `0.25rem` radius. Height is 48px on mobile for thumb reach. Hover: `#121824`. Active: subtle downward translate of 1px.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Cut Trigger):** Terracotta Ochre (`#C85A32`), white text. Used exclusively for cutting phase progression and irreversible atelier triggers.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Parchment fill, 1px border (`#1B2A4A`), indigo text.
- **Bilingual Subtext:** High-priority buttons display primary English action with smaller monospaced Hausa text beneath (e.g., "Cut Pattern" / *Yanka Zane*).

### Badges & Stage Chips
- **Format:** Monospaced 11px uppercase label, `0.25rem` radius, 1px border.
- **Production Stage Badges:**
  - *Cutting / Yanka:* Ochre border, `#C85A32` text, `#FBECE5` fill.
  - *Sewing / Dinki:* Indigo border, `#1B2A4A` text, `#E8EDF5` fill.
  - *Embroidery / Aiki:* Brass border, `#936E20` text, `#FAF4E6` fill.
  - *Fitting / Gwaji:* Cyan border, `#1D596B` text, `#E6F2F5` fill.
  - *QC / Tantance:* Sage border, `#235C3E` text, `#E8F4EC` fill.

### Measurement Inputs & Tables
- **Fields:** Crisp white background with `#E2DDD4` border, inset numeric label in `JetBrains Mono`. Includes physical unit indicator (`cm` or `in`) anchored to the right. Focused state features a `1.5px solid #1B2A4A` outline.
- **Tabular Measurement Grid:** Alternating row highlights between transparent and `#FAF8F3`. Column headers display anatomical markers (Chest, Babban Riga Length, Sleeve, Collar) with unambiguous delta callouts (`+2.0 cm ease`).

### Garment Production Cards
- Compact, multi-tiered white containers edged with `#E2DDD4`. 
- Top header: Client reference, monospaced order ID, and production stage badge.
- Middle body: Garment vector silhouette preview, fabric code, yardage utilization score (e.g., `92.4% nesting efficiency`).
- Bottom row: Mobile-friendly quick actions (assign tailor, initiate cutting sequence, scan bolt RFID).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom square selectors (`0.25rem` radius) with high-contrast indigo fills and sharp checkmarks. Segmented layout switchers (Grid view vs. Cutting list) styled as inset parchment trays with deep indigo active tabs.
